# Top 10 Research Ideas — 2026-07-21

### Idea 1 — Attribute daily P&L by entry weekday, not only exit date
Confidence: High
Reason: All $293.60 realized on Tuesday came from positions opened Monday; existing replay shows Monday-only PF 1.86 and 1.39 at 2x costs versus all-days 1.03 and 0.76. Causal classification: **strategy flaw**. Observation type: **Inferred**.
Test: Add an offline attribution table over the 246-trade panel and post-2026-07-20 clean Bot B sample: entry weekday × exit weekday × gross/net P&L × cost stress.
Data needed: Existing historical ledger plus at least 50 clean post-fix Bot B closes for validation.
Known limitations: Monday-only may select and size differently; historical panel covers one year without a sustained bear market.
Status: New

### Idea 2 — Resolve rejected candidates to full-hold outcomes
Confidence: Moderate
Reason: Bot B's 60-minute mark-outs were seller-favorable in 25/37 cases with mean move -0.0792, but the current window cannot measure TP/stop expectancy. Causal classification: **strategy flaw**. Observation type: **Observed hypothesis trigger**.
Test: Extend offline mark-out resolution to TP, 3x stop, EOD, and expiry outcomes; compare accepted versus rejected candidates with bootstrap confidence intervals and 2x costs.
Data needed: Candidate IDs, strikes, timestamps, entry marks, and complete option-bar paths for at least 500 rejected candidates.
Known limitations: Candidate evaluations are repeated and require deduplication; today's sample is one session.
Status: New

### Idea 3 — Joint 0.25/0.30/0.35-delta sweep under unchanged risk caps
Confidence: Low
Reason: Today's Bot B entries were 0.2758 and 0.2943 delta, while 81 decisions were blocked by 2-ATR gap capacity. Deeper delta targets may trade credit for lower incremental stress. Causal classification: **strategy flaw**. Observation type: **Speculative**.
Test: Replay identical signals at target deltas 0.25, 0.30, and 0.35, enforcing the same credit floors, stop rules, 1/1.5/2-ATR caps, and 2x transaction costs.
Data needed: Same-time full option chains with Greeks and forward option bars for at least 250 signals per target.
Known limitations: Delta, strike depth, credit, skew, and liquidity move together; one-variable interpretation will be imperfect.
Status: New

### Idea 4 — Deeper-cushion efficiency frontier
Confidence: Low

Status: New
